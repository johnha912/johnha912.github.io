AeroPress

What is AeroPress?

The AeroPress, invented by Alan Adler in 2005, is a versatile coffee maker that lets you customize your brew (AeroPress, n.d.). Combining immersion and pressure, it produces a concentrated, smooth coffee with low bitterness (Sprudge, 2020). It supports many techniques and recipes, such as the “classic” and “inverted” methods, and you can adjust grind size, water temperature, and brew time to reach a wide range of flavors. That flexibility, plus its portability, makes it popular with coffee enthusiasts.

Steps

  1. Preheat. Rinse the filter paper with hot water.
  2. Load. Place the filter paper and ground coffee in the AeroPress chamber.
  3. Steep. Pour the hot water over the grounds and stir gently for 30 seconds.
  4. Press. Place the plunger on the chamber and press down firmly until all the water has passed through.
  5. Enjoy. Remove the plunger and enjoy your coffee!

Additional tips

  • Experiment with different beans and grind sizes to find your perfect flavor.
  • For a stronger cup, use more coffee or less water; for a lighter one, use less coffee or more water.
  • Add milk or sugar if you like.
